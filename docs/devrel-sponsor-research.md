## Per company notes

Tavily: small developer tools company that operates its own community rather than buying placements, and it publishes no team page or rate card. Inbound would go through the generic contact or partnership form, so there is no named devrel person to pitch.

Exa: well funded with a structured partner program that explicitly names newsletters, communities, and events as targets, which makes it the strongest fit for a sponsorship pitch. The devrel function is currently unfilled, so the partnerships email and form are the working inbound path.

Browserbase: spends on open source sponsorship and runs its own conference and content engine, but has no public sponsorship program or rate card. Outreach routes through the generic contact and partner forms.

Firecrawl: already pays creators directly through its Ambassadors and affiliate programs, so it has a working budget and process for content sponsorship even though there is no published rate card. A named growth lead exists, which gives a person to address.

Apify: builds its developer audience in house and funds local developer communities, so it reads more like a co-marketing partner than a newsletter or podcast sponsor. It has a named marketing owner and a partnerships inbox.

Nansen: agentic first with deep x402 and MCP documentation and a paid affiliate program, so it already invests in developer channels. No public team page or named devrel owner, so inbound is the general hello email.

Chainlink: runs a large in house pipeline of hackathons, grants, and an advocates program rather than buying placements on a published schedule. Treat it as an ecosystem platform, not a sponsor with a rate card.

## Read across

- Zero of the seven publish a sponsorship rate card. Every inbound is a form or a generic email.
- Exa and Firecrawl are the two most realistic sponsors: both have structured programs aimed at developer creators, and both are x402 or MCP adjacent (Exa has MCP, Firecrawl has MCP).
- Five of the seven run a live x402 or MCP endpoint, so the pitch writes itself: we are building the directory that verifies these endpoints, and their endpoints would be listed and verified in it.
